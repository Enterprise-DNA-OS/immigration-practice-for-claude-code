# File checks and their sources

Checked 28 September 2026. These checks flag missing recorded evidence. They do not certify a file, determine visa eligibility or calculate statutory response periods. The practice retains its professional obligations. A blank finding list means only that these specific checks found no gaps.

## Australia

Source: [OMARA Code, current from 1 March 2022](https://www.mara.gov.au/tools-for-agents-subsite/Files/code-of-conduct-march-2022.pdf), linked by [OMARA's current code page](https://www.mara.gov.au/tools-for-registered-agents/code-of-conduct).

- AU-42 checks for a written service agreement reference, agreement date and fee basis. Section 42 governs service agreements; it includes exceptions this simple check does not assess.
- AU-56 checks application copies and invoice/receipt references against the client-file contents requirement in section 56. A reference cannot prove a document's contents or secure storage.
- AU-56 retention uses seven years after the last action on the file. Any new recorded action extends the floor. Archive dates are review dates, never authority to destroy records.
- Oral file notes support section 55. Operators record the material conversation, author and date. The software cannot detect conversations nobody records.

## New Zealand

Source: [IAA professional practice, Code of Conduct 2014](https://www.iaa.govt.nz/for-advisers/code-of-conduct/professional-practice/).

- NZ-14 flags missing evidence that the adviser supplied licence evidence to the client.
- NZ-18 flags missing agreement, date or fee basis. It checks presence, not whether the agreement meets every requirement.
- NZ-26 flags missing application copies, invoice/receipt references and unconfirmed material oral discussions. Lodgement confirmation is a client update the operator records after actually communicating it.
- NZ-26 retention uses seven years from file closure. Open files have no calculated destruction date. Originals stay in the return queue until their return evidence is recorded.

## Practice checks

PRACTICE-LICENCE compares the assigned adviser's recorded jurisdiction and expiry with the matter and today's date. It is a practice control, not a live register lookup or a determination of exemptions. This version assumes registered AU agents and licensed NZ advisers, not exempt lawyers.

The 14-day quiet-matter and client-update thresholds, 60-day visa-expiry window and evidence-verification gate are configurable practice policies, not legal notice periods. Deadline dates are entered from a specific notice or grant, with its reference. Nothing infers a visa condition, work entitlement, appeal deadline or extension.

The file index references securely stored originals, applications, agreements and correspondence. Back up the database and those files together, restrict access, and verify restore procedures. No client money, trust accounting, payment execution, government lodgement, email or messaging is implemented.
